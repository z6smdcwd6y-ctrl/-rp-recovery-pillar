COMMON QUESTIONS

Honest answers.

Can anyone guarantee my funds will be recovered?

No. Outcomes depend on the circumstances, timing, destination of funds, and decisions made by banks, platforms, exchanges, and authorities.

Should I share my recovery phrase or password?

No. Never share passwords, seed phrases, private keys, or one-time codes with a recovery service.

Do you publish customer testimonials?

Only genuine testimonials authorised by the customer and appropriately verified should be published.

How are prices decided?

Proposed service scope, fees, and terms should be explained clearly in writing before you agree.
