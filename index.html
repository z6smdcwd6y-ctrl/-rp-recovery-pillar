IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTICE

Never share your recovery phrase.

No legitimate helper needs your wallet seed phrase, private keys, account password, or one-time security code. Be cautious of anyone demanding upfront fees, promising guaranteed returns, or asking you to send crypto to “unlock” recovered funds.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can you guarantee that my money will be recovered?

No. Recovery depends on the circumstances, timing, destination of funds, and decisions by banks, exchanges, platforms, or authorities. No result should be promised in advance.

What information should I prepare?

Keep transaction references, dates, payment receipts, wallet addresses, public transaction IDs, messages, and reports. Share sensitive records only through a verified and secure channel.

Should I pay an upfront fee to release recovered funds?

Be very cautious. Requests for extra payments, taxes, or “unlocking” fees tied to a promised recovery are common warning signs. Independently verify any organisation and seek advice from your bank or relevant authority.

Do you need my password or seed phrase?

No. Never provide passwords, private keys, seed phrases, or one-time codes. A public blockchain transaction ID is different from a private key.

How do I verify a service before engaging?

Check the legal business name, registration, physical contact details, applicable authorisation, written terms, privacy policy, and fee structure independently. Do not rely only on claims shown on a website or social profile.
